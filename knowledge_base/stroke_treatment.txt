# Acute Ischemic Stroke Treatment Pathway

## Initial Assessment (Door-to-CT ≤25 minutes)
- ABC assessment and stabilization
- Establish IV access and draw blood (glucose, CBC, coagulation studies, troponin)
- NIHSS (National Institutes of Health Stroke Scale) assessment
- Non-contrast CT head STAT to rule out hemorrhage
- CT angiography (CTA) of head and neck for large vessel occlusion (LVO)
- Blood glucose: Treat if <60 mg/dL; treat hyperglycemia if >180 mg/dL
- Blood pressure management: Permissive hypertension up to 220/120 mmHg (unless thrombolysis planned)

## IV Thrombolysis (Alteplase/Tenecteplase)
### Alteplase (tPA)
- Window: Within 4.5 hours of symptom onset (or last known well)
- Dose: 0.9 mg/kg IV (max 90 mg); 10% as bolus over 1 min, remaining 90% infused over 60 min
- Door-to-needle goal: ≤60 minutes

### Tenecteplase (TNK) — Emerging Alternative
- Single IV bolus: 0.25 mg/kg (max 25 mg)
- Advantages: Simpler administration, may be non-inferior to alteplase

### Key Eligibility Criteria
- Measurable neurological deficit
- CT excludes hemorrhage
- Age ≥18 years
- Blood pressure <185/110 mmHg before treatment

### Major Contraindications
- Active internal bleeding
- Platelet count <100,000
- INR >1.7 or PT >15 seconds
- Ischemic stroke or head trauma within 3 months
- Major surgery within 14 days
- GI or urinary bleeding within 21 days

## Mechanical Thrombectomy
- Indication: Large vessel occlusion (ICA, M1, and in select cases M2 or basilar)
- Window: Up to 24 hours with favorable imaging (perfusion mismatch)
- Standard window: Within 6 hours of symptom onset
- Extended window (6–24 hours): If CT perfusion or MRI shows salvageable brain tissue (DAWN/DEFUSE 3 criteria)
- Stent retriever or aspiration thrombectomy
- Door-to-groin puncture goal: ≤90 minutes

## Post-thrombolysis Care
- Admit to stroke unit or ICU
- Neurological checks every 15 minutes x 2 hours, then every 30 minutes x 6 hours, then hourly x 16 hours
- BP management post-tPA: Maintain <180/105 mmHg for 24 hours
- NO anticoagulants or antiplatelets for 24 hours post-tPA
- Repeat CT at 24 hours before starting antiplatelet therapy
- NPO until dysphagia screen completed

## Secondary Prevention
- Antiplatelet therapy: Aspirin 325 mg within 24–48 hours (after tPA clearance)
  - Dual antiplatelet (aspirin + clopidogrel) for 21 days if minor stroke or high-risk TIA
- Statin: High-intensity statin therapy (atorvastatin 40–80 mg)
- Blood pressure: Target <130/80 mmHg after acute phase
- Atrial fibrillation: Anticoagulation (DOACs preferred) if cardioembolic source
- Carotid revascularization: Endarterectomy or stenting if ≥70% ipsilateral stenosis
- Lifestyle: Smoking cessation, exercise, Mediterranean diet
