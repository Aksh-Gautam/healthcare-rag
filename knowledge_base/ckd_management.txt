# Chronic Kidney Disease (CKD) Management

## Definition and Staging
CKD is defined as abnormalities of kidney structure or function present for >3 months.

### GFR Categories:
- G1: Normal or high GFR (≥90 mL/min/1.73 m²)
- G2: Mildly decreased (60–89)
- G3a: Mildly to moderately decreased (45–59)
- G3b: Moderately to severely decreased (30–44)
- G4: Severely decreased (15–29)
- G5: Kidney failure (<15 or dialysis)

### Albuminuria Categories:
- A1: Normal to mildly increased (<30 mg/g)
- A2: Moderately increased (30–300 mg/g) — "microalbuminuria"
- A3: Severely increased (>300 mg/g) — "macroalbuminuria"

## Blood Pressure Management in CKD
- Target: <120 mmHg systolic (SPRINT trial evidence for non-diabetic CKD)
- For diabetic CKD: <130/80 mmHg
- First-line: ACE inhibitor or ARB (especially with albuminuria ≥30 mg/g)
- DO NOT combine ACE inhibitor + ARB

## Pharmacotherapy to Slow Progression

### RAAS Blockade:
- ACE inhibitor or ARB: For all patients with albuminuria (A2 or A3)
- Monitor potassium and creatinine within 2 weeks of initiation
- Acceptable creatinine rise: ≤30% from baseline

### SGLT2 Inhibitors (NEW CORNERSTONE):
- Dapagliflozin 10 mg or Empagliflozin 10 mg daily
- Indicated: CKD with eGFR ≥20 mL/min (can initiate) regardless of diabetes status
- Benefits: Slows eGFR decline, reduces albuminuria, reduces HF hospitalizations
- DAPA-CKD trial: 39% reduction in sustained eGFR decline or ESKD
- EMPA-KIDNEY trial: 28% reduction in progression of kidney disease

### Finerenone (Non-steroidal MRA):
- For type 2 diabetes with CKD and albuminuria
- Reduces kidney and cardiovascular events (FIDELIO-DKD, FIGARO-DKD trials)
- Dose: 10–20 mg daily based on eGFR
- Monitor potassium closely

## Complication Management

### Anemia:
- Target hemoglobin: 10–11.5 g/dL (avoid >13 g/dL)
- Iron supplementation first: Target ferritin 200–500 ng/mL, TSAT 20–30%
- ESA therapy (epoetin alfa, darbepoetin): After iron optimization
- HIF-PHI (roxadustat, daprodustat): Oral alternative to ESA

### Mineral and Bone Disorder (CKD-MBD):
- Phosphorus management: Dietary restriction, phosphate binders
- Calcium: Avoid calcium-based binders if hypercalcemia or vascular calcification
- Vitamin D: Supplement if 25(OH)D <30 ng/mL
- PTH: Target varies by CKD stage; avoid severe hyperparathyroidism
- Cinacalcet: For secondary hyperparathyroidism in dialysis patients

### Metabolic Acidosis:
- Oral sodium bicarbonate: If serum bicarbonate <22 mEq/L
- Target bicarbonate: 22–26 mEq/L
- Veverimer: Novel acid-reducing agent (emerging therapy)

### Hyperkalemia:
- Dietary potassium restriction
- Sodium polystyrene sulfonate (Kayexalate) — short-term
- Patiromer or sodium zirconium cyclosilicate: For chronic hyperkalemia management

## Dialysis Planning
- Refer to nephrologist: eGFR <30 mL/min
- Vascular access planning: eGFR <25 mL/min (AV fistula preferred, 6 months before anticipated need)
- Initiate dialysis: Based on symptoms, not arbitrary GFR threshold
- Typical initiation: eGFR 5–10 mL/min with uremic symptoms
